Delicious Cafe Food in Ujjain: From Burnt Garlic Maggi to Peri Peri Fries

By Art of Coffee | August 19, 2026 | 4 min read

While coffee is our passion, food is our love language. Take a look at some of the most popular, mouthwatering fast food items available at Art of Coffee Ujjain.

A great cafe experience is complete only when the food matches the quality of the brews. Whether you need a quick study snack, a lunch bowl, or sharing plates for a group hangout, our menu is designed to hit the spot.

The Famous Burnt Garlic Maggi

We took a classic comfort food and elevated it. Our Burnt Garlic Maggi is tossed with smoky charred garlic, fresh herbs, and spices, making it a favorite for students. It is affordable and delicious, perfect for a cozy afternoon date. Read more in our romantic date guide.

Golden Crispy Peri Peri Fries

Served hot and fresh, our fries are dusted with spicy, smoky peri peri seasoning. They are crispy on the outside, soft on the inside, and served with a side of creamy dip. Share a basket during your next group student hangout!
